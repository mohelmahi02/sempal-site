Delete your SemPal account or data

SemPal · developer: SemPal (Mohammad Elmahi and Johnson Mununkum)

Delete your whole account

  1. Open SemPalGo to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete accountIt's at the bottom, under Account. Confirm when asked.

Can't open the app? Email support@sempal.ie from the email address you signed up with, and we'll delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email and profile (college, course, year)
  • Your modules, deadlines, classes, reminders and notes
  • Uploaded syllabus and timetable files
  • Saved calendar links, including your Moodle calendar link
  • Module and timetable shares you created
  • Push notification tokens

This happens straight away. Copies in our backups are removed within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Class alerts and reports you sent are kept without your name for up to 90 days after the class, to deal with abuse reports, then deleted.
  • Crash reports (which don't include your name or email) are kept by our crash reporting service for up to 90 days.

Delete some data but keep your account

  • Deadlines and modules: open the deadline or module and tap Delete.
  • Timetables: Profile → Timetable → the bin icon next to a calendar or upload.
  • Moodle link and its deadlines: Profile → Moodle deadlines → the bin icon.
  • Anything else: email support@sempal.ie and tell us what to delete.
