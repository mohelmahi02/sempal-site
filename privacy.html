Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

SemPal helps students keep track of deadlines and classes. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We never sell your data and we don't show ads.

Who we are

SemPal is run in Ireland by its two founders, Mohammad Elmahi and Johnson Mununkum. Together we are the "controller" of your personal data under the GDPR. When we register a company to run SemPal, it will take over this role and we'll update this policy and tell you.

Contact us at support@sempal.ie.

Class alerts are anonymous to other students. Classmates see what was reported, never who reported it. We can see who sent a report, so we can deal with abuse.

We may share data if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If SemPal is ever sold or merged, your data would move to the new owner under this policy, and we'd tell you first.

International transfers

Some suppliers, including Anthropic, Expo and ImprovMX, process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the supplier is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Our database and files are hosted by Supabase in the EU (Ireland, eu-west-1).

How we keep it safe

Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers. Each student can only access their own data. Our secret keys are only kept on the server, never in the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • See your data: ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Correct it: most of it you can change yourself in the app. Ask us for anything else.
  • Delete it: go to Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and data. Or email us.
  • Take it with you: ask for your data in a format you can reuse.
  • Object or restrict: ask us to stop or limit using your data for things based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw permission: turn off notifications, camera or photo access in your phone's settings at any time.

Email support@sempal.ie to use any of these rights. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie). We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

Age

SemPal is for students aged 16 and over. If you're under 16, please don't use it. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
