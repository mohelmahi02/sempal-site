What SemPal does

SemPal reads your syllabi and timetables, shows your deadlines and classes, sends reminders, and lets classmates share changes to classes.

Always check important dates yourself. We use AI to read your files, and it can make mistakes, such as a wrong date, a missed deadline or a class at the wrong time. Your college's official information, such as your syllabus, Moodle, emails and lecturers' announcements, always comes first. SemPal is a helper, not a replacement for it.

Timetables from your college: when you add a calendar link, we show what your college publishes and check it for changes each day. We can't guarantee your college's timetable is right, or that a change reaches you straight away.

Reminders and notifications depend on your phone's settings and the app being able to sync. They might not arrive, for example if notifications are off, your phone is offline or battery saving stops them.

Class alerts and campus reports

Class alerts and campus reports are written by students for other students. They're only useful if they're honest.

When you post, you agree to:

  • Only report changes you believe are true, such as an email from your lecturer or a notice on the door.
  • Keep notes friendly and relevant to the class.
  • Not post anything abusive, threatening, hateful, sexual or discriminatory, or anything that names or targets another person.
  • Not post spam, pranks, adverts or false reports, including "class cancelled" jokes.
  • Not share anyone's personal information.

Alerts aren't official. They come from classmates, not your college. Check with your college before acting on one, especially before missing a class or an exam.

How we keep it safe:

  • Notes are automatically checked, and ones with swearing or insults are blocked.
  • Anyone with the class can mark an alert Not true, or tap Report if it's abusive or fake. Alerts with enough "Not true" votes or reports are hidden straight away.
  • We review reported alerts and campus reports, usually within 48 hours. We may remove any content that breaks these rules.
  • If your alerts are often taken down, or you break these rules, we may stop you posting alerts or reports, temporarily or permanently. In serious cases we may close your account.
  • Campus-wide notices are only sent after we've checked them.

Your content: you keep ownership of what you post. You give us permission to show it to the students it's meant for, and to store it as described in our Privacy Policy.
