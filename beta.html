Install the SemPal beta

iPhone: open now · Android: coming soon · takes about 3 minutes

Thanks for helping us test! The beta isn't in the App Store or on Google Play for everyone yet, so you'll join through a test link instead. It's the same app, just installed through Apple's or Google's testing system.

iPhone

Join the iPhone beta

  1. Install TestFlightGet Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store. It's how Apple lets people test apps before they're released.
  2. Open the beta link on your iPhoneTap Join the iPhone beta above, or open testflight.apple.com/join/vAXxtfYD.
  3. Tap Accept, then InstallSemPal appears on your home screen like any other app.
  4. Open SemPal and sign upUse your email, pick your college and campus, and allow notifications when asked.
Updates arrive through TestFlight, and smaller fixes download inside the app by themselves. If we ask you to check a fix, close SemPal fully and open it twice.

Android

Coming soon. The Android beta will run through Google Play. To join, send us the Gmail address you use on your phone (in the WhatsApp group, or at support@sempal.ie) and we'll send you the link when it's ready.
Android: Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, Huawei, Oppo, OnePlus or Samsung? These phones can block notifications to save battery. After installing:
  • Long-press the SemPal icon → App info.
  • Battery (or Battery saver) → choose No restrictions / Unrestricted.
  • On Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO, also turn on Autostart.
  • Check Notifications are on.

What to try

  • Upload a syllabus: a photo, a PDF or a Word file. Check the deadlines it finds are right.
  • Add your timetable: from your college calendar link, a PDF or a screenshot. Check the days, times and rooms.
  • Reminders: set one up and see if it arrives on time.
  • Class alerts: report a change to one of your classes, and confirm or mark someone else's.
  • Anything else: tap around, try to break it. Weird files and edge cases are really useful to us.
Keep using your college's official info (Moodle, emails, your lecturers) for anything important. The beta can get things wrong, and that's exactly what we want to hear about.

Tell us what you find

Post in the SemPal beta WhatsApp group, or email support@sempal.ie. The most helpful reports say:

  • what you were doing,
  • what you expected, and what actually happened,
  • a screenshot if you can, and your phone model.

On iPhone you can also take a screenshot while using SemPal and tap Share Beta Feedback. It comes straight to us with your screenshot.

If the app crashes, we get an automatic report, but a quick message about what you were doing still helps a lot.

Please don't post your college calendar link or other private links in the group.

Removing the beta

To delete your data first, go to Profile → Delete account in the app. Then delete the app like any other: long-press the SemPal icon and tap Remove App (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android). Uninstalling on its own doesn't delete your account.
